# MaiSim home page

The MaiSim home page (`/`) provides an overview of your recent simulation runs, quick access to ready-made examples, and package import/export utilities.

## Main cards

- **+ New circuit:** opens a blank schematic sheet in the [circuit editor](#ajuda:simu) to build a new topology.
- **Scope · runs and measurement:** opens the [Scope](#ajuda:scope) to inspect recorded runs, compare waveforms, and calculate spectrums.
- **Examples · ready-made models:** collection of pre-built DC-DC converters, inverters, rectifiers, and closed-loop control circuits.

## Circuit library section

- **Recent and All circuits:** switch between recently modified models and the full directory list.
- **Search:** live filter box to locate circuits by filename or description.
- **Open and Delete:** per-row action buttons to launch the editor or safely trash unwanted files.
- **Export and Import (.msd):** bundled packages carrying the circuit schematic, control source files, and metadata to share with other users.

## The side rail

- **Quick navigation:** jump directly to Home, Simu, Code, and Scope into dedicated popups or windows.
- **AI status:** status dot indicating whether Claude is currently authenticated and available.
- **Interface settings:** scale controls (+ and −), language switcher (PT/EN), and theme selector (system, light, or dark).
- **Help (F1):** the **?** button opens the slide-over quick help panel or standalone reference manual.
